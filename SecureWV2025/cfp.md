Abstract:

Are you passionate about security and technology? Do you thrive on sharing your knowledge with others?
Over my 25-year career in IT, I’ve discovered that embracing the community aspect of tech and openly sharing what I learn has been a game-changer. Throughout my journey, I’ve had to "reinvent" myself multiple times—adapting to industry shifts and evolving job markets—while staying true to my interests. The key to long-term career success? A mindset shift towards continuous learning and community involvement.
By leveraging blogging, speaking, and contributing to professional communities, I’ve not only grown as an expert but also unlocked new career opportunities. In this session, I'll share practical insights on how you can do the same.

#### Key Takeaways:
- **Shift your mindset** toward true job security by focusing on adaptability and learning.
- **Develop a community-centric approach** to build your personal brand and expand your network.
- **Identify the right communities and vendor programs** that align with your interests and professional goals.
If you’re ready to level up your career while making meaningful connections, this session is for you!
